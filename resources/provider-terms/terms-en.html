Basis and scope of the application fee

  1. 1. The application fee is based on section 8 a of the Universities Act (558/2009) and section 12 a of the Universities of Applied Sciences Act (932/2014).
  2. 2. The application fee is EUR 100 in accordance with section 1 of the Decree of the Ministry of Education and Culture on the chargeable services of the Finnish National Agency for Education and its independent units (1508/2025).
  3. 3. The recipient of the application fee is the Finnish National Agency for Education (2769790-1).
  4. 4. The application fee is a public-law fee charged by the Finnish National Agency for Education and is subject to the public-law procedures for appeal. If you believe that an error has been made in determining the application fee, you should first contact the Finnish National Agency for Education's advisory service for application fees. You may submit a written request for rectification concerning the application fee to the Finnish National Agency for Education in accordance with the Act on Criteria for Charges Payable to the State.
  5. 5. The application fee covers the processing of the application and is not used to pay tuition, tuition fees or other study-related fees.

Payment and validity

  1. 6. The application fee must be paid no later than seven (7) days from the submission of the application. The application fee cannot be paid after the due date, and late payments will not be accepted.
  2. 7. The applicant is responsible for ensuring that the application fee is paid within the deadline and in accordance with the instructions given via the payment link provided for that purpose.
  3. 8. The application fee can also be paid by a person other than the applicant. However, the applicant is responsible for ensuring that the payment is made correctly in accordance with the instructions, within the deadline and that it is applied to the correct application.
  4. 9. The application fee is deemed to have been paid once the payment has been received and registered in the payment system.
  5. 10. A receipt of successful payment will be sent to the email address provided by the applicant. The receipt shows the start period for which the application fee has been paid. It is the applicant's responsibility to provide a valid email address.
  6. 11. The application fee is only valid for the start term of the study programme for which the fee has been paid. The application fee cannot be transferred to another start term.
  7. 12. One application fee covers all of the applicant's applications to study programmes starting in the same start term. However, the application fee covers only those applications for the same start term whose payment deadline has not expired at the time the fee is paid. The application fee cannot be used to process applications whose payment deadline has already expired before the fee is paid.
  8. 13. The applicant is responsible for any fees and charges charged by banks, credit card companies, mobile payment services or other payment service providers.

Effect on Application Processing

  1. 14. The application fee is a prerequisite for processing the application for those applicants who are liable to pay the application fee. If the application fee is not paid within the time limit, the application will not be processed.
  2. 15. Paying the application fee does not affect the outcome of the student selection process.
  3. 16. The approved payment of the application fee is not a statement on the applicant's eligibility to apply, eligibility for admission or chances of being admitted.
  4. 17. The application fee will not be refunded even if the applicant withdraws their application, does not participate in the admission process, does not receive a study place or is found to be ineligible to apply.
  5. 18. The application fee will not be refunded or deducted from any tuition fees or other study-related fees.

Applicant's responsibilities and verification of information

  1. 19. The applicant is responsible for the correctness, timeliness and completeness of all the information provided in the application.
  2. 20. The applicant is responsible for ensuring that the information on his or her nationality, residence permit or other grounds for exemption is correct and verifiable.
  3. 21. If the applicant declares that he or she is exempt from the obligation to pay the application fee, he or she must submit the required documents within the time limit. The document exempting from the application fee must be valid in accordance with the admissions criteria and submitted within the deadline.
  4. 22. If the document proving the grounds for exemption is not submitted within the time limit or the conditions for exemption are not met, the applicant may be considered liable to pay an application fee.
  5. 23. Incorrect, incomplete or misleading information may result in an application fee being charged or the application not being processed.
  6. 24. The obligation to pay the application fee is assessed on the basis of the information and documents provided by the applicant and information held by authorities.
  7. 25. The applicant accepts that information related to liability to pay the application fee and the grounds for exemption from the application fee may be verified with the competent authorities. The information to be checked may include, for example, information on the applicant's nationality, right of residence, the validity of the residence permit, the grounds and type of residence permit, and the status of the processing of the residence permit matter.
  8. 26. The final assessment of the liability to pay the application fee can be made during the processing of the application. If, on the basis of the information received later, it appears that the applicant does not meet the conditions for exemption from the application fee, the applicant may be considered liable to pay the application fee.
  9. 27. The terms and conditions of the application fee are determined in accordance with the legislation in force at the time.
