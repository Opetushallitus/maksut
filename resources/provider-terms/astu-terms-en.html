Terms for the decision fee

By accepting these terms and conditions, I confirm that I understand and agree to the following terms regarding the decision fee.

Basis and scope of the decision fee

1. The decision fee is based on the following acts:

- Act on the recognition of professional qualifications (1384/2015, section 13 a)

- Act on eligibility for public posts provided by higher education studies completed abroad (1385/2015, section 6 a)

2. The decision fee is EUR 395. The decision fee is based on the following decree:

- Decree of the Ministry of Education and Culture on the chargeable services of the Finnish National Agency for Education and its independent units (1508/2025, section 1, subsections 4 and 5; section 2, subsections 1 and 2)

  1. 3. The recipient of the decision fee is the Finnish National Agency for Education (2769790-1).
  2. 4. The decision fee is a public-law fee charged by the Finnish National Agency for Education and is subject to the public-law procedures for appeal.
  3. 5. For enquiries regarding the decision fee, the applicant may contact the Finnish National Agency for Education at recognition@oph.fi.

Payment and validity

  1. 6. The decision fee is deemed to have been paid once the payment has been received and registered in the payment system.
  2. 7. The Finnish National Agency for Education sends a receipt of a successful payment to the email address provided by the applicant. It is the applicant's responsibility to provide a valid email address.
  3. 8. The applicant is responsible for any fees and charges charged by banks, credit card companies, mobile payment services, or other payment service providers.

Effect on receiving the decision

  1. 9. The Finnish National Agency for Education will send the decision to the applicant once the applicant has paid the decision fee.
  2. 10. If the applicant does not pay the decision fee within 14 days, the Finnish National Agency for Education will send the applicant a separate invoice regarding the decision fee. The payment is enforceable without a judgement or a decision (Act on Criteria for Charges Payable to the State 150/1992, section 11, subsection 1).
