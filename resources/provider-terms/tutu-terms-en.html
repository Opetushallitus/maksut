Terms for the processing fee

By accepting these terms and conditions, I confirm that I understand and agree to the following terms regarding the processing fee.

Basis and scope of the processing fee

1. The processing fee is based on the following acts:

- Act on the recognition of professional qualifications (1384/2015, section 13 a)

- Act on eligibility for public posts provided by higher education studies completed abroad (1385/2015, section 6 a)

2. The processing fee is EUR 100. The fee is based on the following decree:

- Decree of the Ministry of Education and Culture on the chargeable services of the Finnish National Agency for Education and its independent units (1508/2025, section 1, subsection 23)

  1. 3. The recipient of the processing fee is the Finnish National Agency for Education (2769790-1).
  2. 4. The processing fee is a public-law fee charged by the Finnish National Agency for Education and is subject to the public-law procedures for appeal.
  3. 5. For enquiries regarding the processing fee, the applicant may contact the Finnish National Agency for Education at recognition@oph.fi.

Payment and Validity

  1. 6. The processing fee is deemed to have been paid once the payment has been received and registered in the payment system.
  2. 7. The Finnish National Agency for Education sends a receipt of a successful payment to the email address provided by the applicant. It is the applicant's responsibility to provide a valid email address.
  3. 8. The applicant is responsible for any fees and charges charged by banks, credit card companies, mobile payment services, or other payment service providers.

Effect on the processing of the application

  1. 9. The Finnish National Agency for Education will begin to process the application once the applicant has paid the processing fee. If the applicant does not pay the processing fee within 14 days of submitting the application, the application will expire and it will not be processed.
  2. 10. The Finnish National Agency for Education will not return the processing fee to the applicant even if the applicant cancels the application.
  3. 11. If the applicant has filled in the application form for the recognition of qualification, the applicant must later pay a separate decision fee before receiving the decision. When the Finnish National Agency has made the decision, the applicant will receive the instructions for paying the decision fee by email.
  4. 12. If the applicant has filled in the application form for a final decision, the Finnish National Agency for Education will not charge the applicant a separate decision fee.
